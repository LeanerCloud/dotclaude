# Germany and draft-return checklist

Use for a German return, particularly freelance records and EÜR. Verify applicable rules for the filing year using BMF guidance, official legislation, Finanzverwaltung/ELSTER instructions and official certificates. Software help explains fields but does not replace tax authority. No rates, ceilings or eligibility decisions from an earlier chat carry forward automatically.

## Freelance income and EÜR

Establish actual accounting and VAT status. Not charging VAT, foreign clients, or low domestic receipts alone do not prove Kleinunternehmer eligibility or determine domestic, exempt, non-taxable or reverse-charge treatment. Confirm the year's rules and each supply's facts before mapping income. Keep customer location, supply type and VAT treatment distinct from ledger categories.

Tie EÜR totals to the validated ledger and document adjustments separately. Check business travel, home office, equipment/depreciation, training versus other expenses, accommodation, advertising and fees where applicable. A vendor category does not prove a form-field assignment. Do not expense an asset and depreciate it again, or restart a prior-year asset's full cost.

For travel, obtain confirmed dates, locations and business purpose. Separate paid costs from meal allowances. Match cancellations and refunds, calculate eligible arrival/departure/full days, account for supplied meals, and verify the year's destination schedule. Protect confirmed travel and allowance records from generic categorization. Do not classify every flight or lodging transaction as business travel.

## Certificates, prepayments and tax-year attribution

Use the relevant year's health/pension and withholding certificates when available. Separate qualifying contributions from other debits/refunds; deductible certificate figures may differ from bank totals.

Inspect payment purposes and assessment/reference years. A following-year payment can be explicitly attributed to a prior-year prepayment. Do not infer no prepayments from absence in that year's filtered ledger. Verify the assessment and payment evidence, distinguish prepayments from settlements and withholding, enter the applicable amount once, and avoid treating income-tax payments as business costs. Verify taxpayer identifiers against the owner's authoritative documents, not nearby bank metadata.

Check investment income against certificates and statements. No securities sales does not prove no interest, dividends, withholding or other reportable events. Distinguish an owner-directed draft change from a verified zero, and state missing evidence.

## Family, childcare, school and household expenses

Obtain separation dates, household arrangements, child's residence/visits, benefit recipient and entitlement, payer and agreements only as relevant. Do not infer joint assessment, single-parent relief or child allocation from a single fact such as receiving benefits or paying the bill.

Childcare, school fees and household services are different regimes. Separate qualifying services from meals, accommodation and excluded components using invoices and current rules. Verify minijob evidence and employer costs where relevant. Do not claim the same service twice.

Paying the full bill does not automatically establish a right to the other parent's allocation. Verify rules and required consent; keep pending agreements visible. Do not duplicate full amounts across parents' returns. Check the selected period against the actual arrangement rather than automatically limiting it to months after separation.

Rent or a statement that services are included does not establish an eligible household-services amount. Seek the landlord/service-charge certificate or required evidence. Keep missing evidence unresolved rather than inventing a deduction or asserting no opportunity exists.

## SteuerGo or another draft application

Verify return/year before editing. Review carried-forward values field by field. In SteuerGo, highlighted previous-year numbers need confirmation. Itemized calculators may require blur, save or confirmation before totals update; verify saved output rather than trusting typing success.

Keep a compact mapping of section/field, evidence, raw amount, entered amount, software-applied deduction and unresolved conditions. Verify rounding and whether a field expects an eligible gross amount or an already reduced deduction. Do not apply a percentage twice. Revisit lines after removal/replacement because indices can shift.

Inspect details when headers appear stale. Run check-data and distinguish errors from warnings. Download the draft preview, verify year/version and inspect income, expenses, family fields, withholding/prepayments and result. Retain missing certificates or allocation agreements in the handoff. Preparation alone does not authorize filing, payment or third-party communications.

## Estimates and payment arrangements

For joint versus separate scenarios, verify eligibility and full-year treatment instead of mechanically prorating a joint tariff by months together. Use actual income, deductions and household facts. Keep estimates separate from the saved filing choice.

Separate next-year YTD actuals, confirmed future receipts, explicitly labelled assumptions and prepayments. Confirm currency/magnitude after corrections. Do not invent replacement receipts or leave superseded forecasts active.

For instalments or deferral, verify current eligibility, interest, deadlines and process against the assessment. Draft only when asked. Name missing facts/documents and do not promise approval or submit without authorization.
